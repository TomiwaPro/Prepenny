# You can change one payment, or every later one too

Tap a payment, on Home or on Coming up, to change it, move it or skip it. Every change can be undone straight away.

Spending money from a budget is changed on **Budgets**, under More, instead: its sheet says so.

## Quick changes

- **Mark as paid** once it has gone from your bank. For money coming in, it's **Mark as arrived**.
- **Move to next payday**, for a payment going out, moves it to the first payday after its date, and says the date first, such as "Moves it to Fri 6 Nov". It's there once you have a main pay.
- **Skip this one** leaves out just this payment.

## Changing the amount, date or name

Under **Change it**, type the new amount, date, name or note, then choose:

- **Just this one**: only this payment changes. Rent for one month, say.
- **This and all later ones**: this payment and every one after it change. Earlier ones stay as they were.

If your pay goes round in a pattern and you change its amount for this and all later ones, PrePenny also asks which paydays get the new amount: **Every payday from now**, or only this one in the pattern.

Then tap **Save**.

## Removing a payment

Tap **Remove**, then **Remove just this one** or **Remove this and all later ones**.

## Taking a change back

After every change, a bar at the bottom of the screen says what changed, with **Undo**. One tap puts everything back as it was. The bar stays until your next change, or until you go to another screen.

Once you're signed in, you can take back an older change of your own too: go to **More**, then **Activity**, and tap **Undo this change** under it. It's there as long as nothing it changed has been changed since.

## Trying a change first

**What if**, under More, lets you try changes without touching your real plan. You see the new lowest point beside the real one, then keep the changes or throw them away.
