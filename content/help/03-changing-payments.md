# You can change one payment, or every later one too

Tap any payment, on Home or on Coming up, to change it, move it or skip it. Every change can be undone.

## Quick changes

- **Mark as paid** once it has gone from your bank. For money coming in, it's **Mark as arrived**.
- **Move to next payday** moves it to your next payday, and says the date first.
- **Skip this one** leaves out just this payment.

## Changing the amount, date or name

Under **Change it**, type the new amount, date, name or note, then choose:

- **Just this one**: only this payment changes. Rent for one month, say.
- **This and all later ones**: this payment and every one after it change. Earlier ones stay as they were.

Then tap **Save**.

If your pay goes round in a pattern, PrePenny asks which paydays get the new amount: every payday from now, or only this one in the pattern.

## Removing a payment

Under **Remove it**, choose **Remove just this one** or **Remove this and all later ones**.

## Taking a change back

After every change, a bar at the bottom of the screen says what changed, with **Undo**. One tap puts everything back as it was.

Once you're signed in, you can take back an older change too: go to **More**, then **Activity**, and tap **Undo this change** under it.

## Trying a change first

**What if**, under More, lets you try changes without touching your real plan. You see the new lowest point beside the real one, then keep the changes or throw them away.
