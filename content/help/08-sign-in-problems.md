# There are ways back in if you can't sign in

PrePenny signs you in with a 6-digit code sent to your email address, or with Google. There's no password to forget.

## The code hasn't arrived

- Look in your junk or spam folder for an email from PrePenny.
- Check the email address is right. If it isn't, tap **Use a different email address**.
- A code lasts 10 minutes. Tap **Send a new code** for another.
- If too many codes have been sent, PrePenny says how long to wait.

You can also tap the link in the email, on the same phone.

## The code doesn't work

Type the 6 numbers from the newest email from PrePenny. If it has run out, tap **Send a new code**.

## You can't get into your email

If you added a backup email, under **More**, then **Email addresses**, you can use it to get back in. On the sign-in page, tap **Can't get into your email? Use your backup email**.

For your safety, this takes 24 hours. The first code starts the wait, and PrePenny tells the address you sign in with now, so it can be stopped if it wasn't you. After 24 hours, a new code finishes it, and your backup email becomes the address you sign in with.

No backup email? Write to [hello@prepenny.co.uk](mailto:hello@prepenny.co.uk) from any address, and say which address you signed up with.

## You've lost your authenticator app

If two-step sign-in is on and you lose the phone with your authenticator app, you can't sign in. Before you change phones, turn two-step sign-in off, or keep its key somewhere safe. If it's already lost, write to [hello@prepenny.co.uk](mailto:hello@prepenny.co.uk) from the address you sign in with.

## Signed out without meaning to

Someone signed in to your account on another phone can sign this one out, with **Sign out of all other devices**. Your plan is saved in your account, so it comes back when you sign in again. If it wasn't you, look at **Signed-in devices**, under **More**.
