# A shared plan shows your partner only what you choose

Two people can plan money together, each on their own phone. You choose which accounts your partner sees, and the rest stay private.

## Inviting your partner

You both need an account, and the person who made the plan invites. Go to **More**, then **Share with my partner**. Under **Invite your partner**:

- Type your name, as your partner will see it.
- Choose **Can edit** or **Can view only**. You can change it later.
- Make the invite link and send it to your partner, or have PrePenny email it. It lasts 7 days and works once.
- When someone asks to join, PrePenny asks you first, with the email address they signed in with. Tap **Allow** only if it's your partner. Nobody joins without you.

## What your partner sees

Your partner sees each account you share: its balance and every payment in it. Choose them with **Choose shared accounts**. Your savings goals and debts stay private unless you share them, and your job details always do. Whoever shared an account can make it private again later.

A change either of you makes shows on the other's phone within seconds while PrePenny is open there with a signal, or the next time it opens. Each payment's sheet says who changed it last, and **Activity** lists every change you can see, by either of you. Changes to what only one of you sees show only to them.

## Leaving

Either of you can leave at any time, from **Share with my partner**, then **Leave this plan**. Nobody has to agree. Everything only you see comes with you into a plan of your own, and you choose whether to take a copy of the shared accounts. Every shared account stays with your partner as it is.

## If someone controls your money

Money can be used to control someone: their money, and the things money buys, such as food, transport and a place to live. Surviving Economic Abuse has information and support for anyone this is happening to: [survivingeconomicabuse.org](https://survivingeconomicabuse.org/i-need-help/).

If you're in danger now, [call the police on 999](tel:999).

Free helplines, from Surviving Economic Abuse's website:

- National Domestic Abuse Helpline, 24 hours a day: [0808 2000247](tel:08082000247)
- Men's Advice Line: [0808 801 0327](tel:08088010327)
- Galop, for LGBTQ+ people: [0800 999 5428](tel:08009995428)

PrePenny never shows your partner when you last opened it. On a phone someone else can pick up, **App lock**, under **More**, asks for a PIN when PrePenny opens.
