# Each debt shows what's left and when it's paid off

The **Debts** tab shows what you owe now, and the date each debt will be paid off at the payments you've planned.

## Adding a debt

Tap **Add a debt**, on the Debts tab or in setup. It can be a pay-later plan, a credit card, a loan, an overdraft, or money owed to a person. Type what you owe now, and how you pay it.

## Linking payments

A payment pays off a debt when it's linked to it. In **Add a payment**, choose the debt under **Paying off a debt, or saving towards a goal**. Each linked payment then comes off what's left.

## What each debt shows

Tap a debt to see:

- **Left now**: what you still owe.
- **Paid off on**: the date the last planned payment clears it. If it isn't cleared within 24 months, it says what will still be left then.
- Every payment, with what's left after it.

If a payment is more than what's left, it says so, with a button to make it the right amount and stop after it.

## Keeping it right

When a statement comes, tap **Update from statement** and type what it says you owe. Tick any payment it already shows, so it comes off once.

## Credit cards

With an interest rate and statement day, PrePenny adds an estimate of the interest each month, and works out the minimum from your card's rule. **Compare ways to pay** shows how many payments it takes to clear the card, and the interest on the way, for the minimum, a fixed amount and all you owe. It shows the numbers, not which to choose.

## Other currencies

For money owed in euros or US dollars, you set the rate. PrePenny shows both currencies.
