# Your plan starts from what your bank shows today

PrePenny works forward from what your banking app shows now, and every payment coming up. Setting it up takes about 5 minutes.

## Making your plan

Tap **Make my plan**. There are 6 screens, in 3 parts:

1. **Money in**: your account, then how you're paid.
2. **Money out**: home and bills, debts, then spending money.
3. **Your plan**: what PrePenny worked out from what you typed.

Only your account is needed. Every other screen has **Skip this step**, and you can add or change anything later.

## What to have ready

- What your banking app shows today, for each account you want to plan.
- When you're next paid, and about how much.
- Your rent or mortgage, bills and debt payments, with their amounts and dates.

Not sure of an amount? Type your best guess and tick **This is an estimate**. You can put the real amount in when it arrives.

## After setup

**Here's your plan** shows what PrePenny worked out, such as your lowest point ahead. From then on, update your balance when you check your bank, and tick payments as they go out.

## Just looking

**Look at an example plan** opens a made-up plan, so you can look around first. Nothing in it is yours, and any changes you make to it stay on your phone.

## Your plan is on your phone

Until you sign up, your plan is kept only on this phone. To keep it in an account, so it comes with you to another phone, go to **More**, then **Sign up or sign in**. There's no password: PrePenny emails you a 6-digit code.
