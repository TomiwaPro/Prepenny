# Deleting your account removes everything, for good

You can delete your account and every bit of your plan at any time, from **More**, under **Your data**. It can't be undone.

## Keep a copy first, if you want one

**Download my data**, in the same place, saves your plan as a spreadsheet and as a backup file. **Import a backup** can bring a backup file back later, in place of your plan.

## Deleting your account

1. Go to **More**, then **Delete my account and all my data**.
2. To check it's you, PrePenny emails a 6-digit code to the address you sign in with. If two-step sign-in is on, it asks for your authenticator app's code too.
3. Type **delete** to confirm, then tap **Delete my account and all my data**.

## What's deleted

Your plan, with every account, payment, debt, budget and savings goal, and its history. Your signed-in devices, your backup email, your two-step sign-in and your sign-in itself. Your other phones remove everything of yours within a minute. An email tells you it's done.

It's deleted from PrePenny straight away. Copies in PrePenny's backups, and in the records of the services that run it, are gone within 30 days.

## In a shared plan

Deleting your account takes you out of the plan first, as **Leave this plan** does. Every shared account stays with your partner as it is, and everything only you see is deleted. Your changes stay in your partner's Activity, as made by "Someone who has left", without your name or email address.

## Without an account

If you haven't signed up, your plan is only on this phone. Go to **More**, then **Delete my account and all my data**, and tap **Remove everything from this phone**. Your plan, and everything else PrePenny keeps on this phone, goes for good.
