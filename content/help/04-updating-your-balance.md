# Updating your balance keeps your plan true to your bank

When you check your banking app, tell PrePenny what it shows. Everything ahead then starts from the right amount.

## How to update it

1. On Home, tap **Update balance**.
2. If you have more than one account, choose it. Type what your bank shows now. If it's overdrawn, tick **It's overdrawn, below £0.00**. Tap **Next**.
3. PrePenny shows what the plan expected by the end of today, and the difference, such as "That's £4.53 less." Tap **Next**.
4. Tick anything from today or earlier that has gone out or come in. **Tick all** ticks them all. Tap **Done**.

PrePenny then shows your new lowest point ahead. If it wasn't what you meant, **Undo** takes it all back.

## Why ticking matters

Your bank's figure already has the payments that have gone. Ticking them tells PrePenny not to take them off again. Anything you leave unticked is still to come off, or come in.

## When the figures differ

A difference isn't a mistake. It may be a payment you didn't plan, pay that came in different, or a bill that was more or less than expected. Your new balance is the one PrePenny now works from.

## How often

Whenever you look at your bank is a good time. Payday is a good habit: once you've signed up, PrePenny can remind you, under **More**, then **Reminders**.
