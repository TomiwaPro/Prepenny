# Help when money is tight

If your plan shows you'll be short, you don't have to sort it out on your own. These charities give free, confidential advice about debt and money.

## StepChange

Free debt advice for anyone in the UK, online at any time or by phone.

- Call [0800 138 1111](tel:08001381111), free from mobiles and landlines. Monday to Friday 8am to 8pm, Saturday 9am to 2pm.
- Or start online, any time: [stepchange.org](https://www.stepchange.org/setting-expectations.aspx).

Their calls may show as "No Caller ID", so nobody else who picks up your phone knows they're in touch.

## National Debtline

Free debt advice by phone or webchat, for people in England, Wales and Scotland.

- Call [0808 808 4000](tel:08088084000), free to call. Monday to Friday 9am to 8pm, Saturday 9:30am to 1pm.
- Or [chat online](https://nationaldebtline.org/get-help/webchat/).

## Citizens Advice

Free advice about debt, and much more.

- Debt helpline: [0800 240 4420](tel:08002404420), free from mobiles and landlines. Monday to Friday 9am to 8pm, Saturday 9:30am to 1pm, but not on public holidays.
- Or [chat online about debt](https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/about-us/information/chat-with-us-online-about-debt/), at the same times.
- If you can't hear or speak on the phone, use Relay UK: dial 18001 then 0800 240 4420.
- In Northern Ireland, Citizens Advice's website [points you to Advice NI](https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/about-us/northern-ireland/) for debt help.

## If someone else controls your money

[Surviving Economic Abuse](https://survivingeconomicabuse.org/i-need-help/) has information and support for anyone whose partner or ex-partner controls their money. If you're in danger now, [call the police on 999](tel:999).

## In PrePenny

Your plan shows when you'd be short, and by how much. You can tap a payment to move it to a later payday, change it or skip it, and see your new lowest point straight away. Every change can be undone straight away. **What if**, under More, lets you try changes before you make them.
