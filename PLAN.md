## Waiting for the owner

Questions that need an answer before the step that uses them. Move each one to `docs/DECISIONS.md` once it's answered.

1. **Closing an account with money in it (D151)** (before 2f, which builds the account screens). An account can be closed once nothing is planned in it, but the engine doesn't look at what's left in it: an account closed with £50.00 in it keeps that £50.00 in its history and is hidden. The proposal: an account can only be closed when the plan shows £0.00 in it at the end of today, so money never disappears from view; the person moves it out or updates the balance first. Say if that's right, or if closing with money in it is fine.
2. **"The last day" in setup's question about pay (D142)** (before 2e, which builds setup). When the last day of work picked is the last day of a month, for example "Fri 5 Dec pays for work up to Mon 30 Nov", the engine keeps "the last day of the month", so January's pay covers work to Thu 31 Dec, and March's to Sun 28 Feb. Any other day is kept as that day: "up to Sat 28 Nov" means the 28th every month. Say if that's right, or if a month's last day should be kept as its number, the 30th.
3. **Linking a payment to a budget (D153)** (before 2d, which builds Add a payment). ARCHITECTURE.md lists a linked budget on a payment, beside a linked debt or goal. But a budget already puts its own money in the plan (D107), and spending from it is recorded with "I spent", so a payment linked to a budget would count the same money twice, or need a new rule. The proposal: payments link to debts and goals only. Say if that's right, or what a payment linked to a budget should do.
4. **Changing a budget part-way through (D152)** (before 2f, which builds the budget sheet). A new amount counts from the period with today in it; periods that have ended keep what they had. So Sam's Travel, £25.00 with £7.00 left last week, changed to £30.00 on Sun 25 Oct, is £30.00 + £7.00 this week, not £30.00 + £12.00. And a budget with spends or bills can't change to or from a pay-later bill: it's ended and a new one added. Say if both are right.
5. **Overtime after a pay week changes (D154)** (before 2d, which builds I did overtime). When a pay profile or pay week changes, for example a new tax code or this week's "year so far" from a payslip, every overtime and missed shift saved on it is worked out again, so it matches the week it's worked out from. With 11.5 hours on Sam's Fri 6 Nov, changing his 4-shift week from 46 to 40 hours changes what the overtime adds. The proposal is what's built: work them out again. Say if they should keep the figure from when they were saved instead.
